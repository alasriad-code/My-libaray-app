# My Library – Installable on Android

This is a Progressive Web App (PWA) version of your book tracker.

## New Features
- **Scan ISBN with camera**: Point your phone camera at the barcode on the back of a book. It looks up the title, author, and cover automatically (requires Chrome on Android + camera permission).
- **Search online**: Type a book name or author and pick from results to auto-fill the form.
- After filling, choose whether it’s a **Physical**, **Ebook**, or **Both**, then save.

## How to install on Android

1. Host these files somewhere with HTTPS (required for camera + install).
   - Free options: GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or any static host.
   - Upload the entire `my-library-pwa` folder contents.
2. Open the URL in **Chrome** on your Android phone.
3. Tap the menu (⋮) → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. The app will appear on your home screen and open in its own window (no browser bar).

Your books stay saved in the browser’s local storage on that device.
